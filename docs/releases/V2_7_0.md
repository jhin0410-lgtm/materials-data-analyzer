# v2.7.0 - Evidence-Line Closeout and Cross-Repository Integration

Release date: 2026-07-28

## Overview

v2.7.0 closes the complete post-v2.4 repository scope as one additive stable
release. It includes the internal v2.5 compatibility and retrieval-
reproducibility stages, the checksum-bound Battery v2.6.1-v2.6.14 evidence line,
and 38 subsequent integration and public-repository commits.

The previously considered v2.6.0 public candidate is superseded. The tracked
v2.6.14 closeout explicitly declares the v2.6 evidence line closed and forbids
an automatic v2.6.15 stage. The later characterization handoff, public producer-
consumer workflows, representative NIST workflow, repository hardening,
citation governance, and release-readiness audits form a distinct additive
minor-release boundary. Assigning them to v2.6.0 would erase that recorded
closeout boundary.

No separate v2.5.0 or v2.6.0 public release is created. Internal stage labels are
preserved as development history inside v2.7.0.

v2.2.4 structure summary and Battery v2.3.5 source-lineage summary. It performs
deterministic raw-byte and canonical logical-content checks while preserving

## Battery v2.6 benchmark and evidence line

### Registered warm-start benchmark

v2.6.1 evaluates a five-cycle capacity-retention forecast using battery-disjoint
GroupKFold validation, train-only preprocessing, a persistence baseline, and a
fixed Ridge baseline.

The actual Ridge improvement claim is **Unsupported**:

- Ridge pooled MAE: `4.1537`;
- persistence pooled MAE: `3.4256`;
- Ridge improves on 13 of 33 evaluated batteries.

The scenario is warm-start cross-battery forecasting, not zero-shot. It does not
support SOH, RUL, lifetime, mechanism, calibrated probability, or engineering-
decision claims.

### Failure diagnostics and comparability

v2.6.2 preserves the benchmark and adds deterministic failure diagnostics. The
top five batteries account for `90.35%` of Ridge excess absolute error, but no
leave-one-battery-out exclusion reverses persistence superiority. These results
do not justify retuning, clipping, target redefinition, or battery exclusion.

v2.6.3 records `comparability_not_established`. Chemistry, nominal capacity,
cycle-specific commanded protocols, cutoff policy, calibration/uncertainty, and
the official NASA snapshot remain unresolved or heterogeneous.

### External-cohort admission and SNL evidence

v2.6.4 records:

- inventory review: `admitted_with_restrictions`;
- cross-cohort comparability: `not_admitted`;
- predictive validation: `blocked`;
- overall: `not_admitted_for_cross_cohort_validation`.

v2.6.5-v2.6.10 then perform progressively bounded SNL LFP source-document
recovery, local ZIP identity and safe-entry binding, repository nomenclature

These stages establish useful document, archive, schema, and candidate-regime

snapshot, cross-cohort comparability, or predictive-validation eligibility.

### Michigan source path and final closeout

v2.6.11 selects Michigan Formation for source binding only, not cohort admission.
v2.6.12 recovers provider-package structure but not an exact file manifest or
provider-to-local binding. v2.6.13 records an observed provider metadata-access
HTTP 403 without turning it into a claim of global Deep Blue unavailability.

v2.6.14 verifies all 13 tracked v2.6 stage artifacts in order with canonical
checksums and closes the evidence line. Final decisions:

- evidence-line integrity: `verified`;
- registered NASA warm-start benchmark: `preserved`;
- Ridge generalization: `unsupported`;
- cross-cohort comparability: `not_established`;
- external-cohort admission: `not_admitted`;
- predictive-validation readiness: `not_ready`;
- provider-to-local binding: `not_established`;
- engineering-decision readiness: `not_ready`;
- final evidence-line scientific status: **Inconclusive**.

The strongest evidence is the complete checksum-bound chain. The primary
limitation is missing source-backed compatible conditions and provider-to-local
binding, not insufficient model complexity. No automatic v2.6.15 stage is
authorized.

## Cross-repository characterization handoff

The data repository consumes schema `1.0` bundles from the independently
installable `materials-characterization-analyzer` repository.

The handoff validates explicit `sample_id` rather than row order, bundle paths,
sizes and hashes, exact feature columns, finite numeric values, semantic feature
identity, source and preprocessing provenance, sample context, external process-
table identity fields, one-to-one integration, and deterministic reports and
manifests.

Pinned public workflows cover DWCNT, RWGS XRD/EDS, four carbon material classes,
and NIST AM-Bench optical metrology. Blocked SEM quantification, unresolved Ni
context, distinct material-class comparisons, and source-reported microscopy
limitations remain explicitly preserved.

These workflows validate software contracts and evidence lineage. They do not
prove phase identity, chemical state, identical aliquots, mechanism, causality,
prediction, optimization, or engineering-release suitability.

## Representative NIST AM-Bench workflow

The representative workflow performs:

```text
verified source-table case
-> sample-ID and provenance integration
-> artifact-integrity closeout
-> process-design identifiability audit
-> bounded minimum next-experiment plan
-> Diagnostic scientific closeout
```

The evidence contains one material, one machine, 10 explicit tracks, and three
coupled power-speed conditions. It covers 3 of 6 combinations in the observed
2 x 3 grid.

The decision remains `not_ready_for_predictive_or_causal_modeling`. Interaction
and curvature are not identified by the existing design. The immediate bounded
recommendation is to add the three missing factorial combinations with at least
three traces per condition. No response model is trained and no process optimum
is generated.

Official commanded-to-actual power and laser-spot-size corrections are retained
as provenance. They do not create additional process conditions, remeasure the
microscopy data, or turn the line-energy descriptor into volumetric energy
density.

## Compatibility

v2.7.0 is additive and preserves existing valid CLI commands, loaders, tracked
output schemas, source tables, and local-only artifact policies. The two
materials repositories remain independently installable and exchange versioned
files instead of importing each other's internal modules.

## Security and artifact policy

The release does not intentionally include credentials, private datasets, raw
archives lacking redistribution approval, generated `outputs/`, user-specific
absolute paths, arbitrary serialized Python objects, or local row-level
prediction artifacts.

External datasets, publications, standards, and software retain their own
licenses and citation requirements. The repository MIT license does not
relicense them.

## Validation boundary

consistency, checksum-bound evidence, and tracked cross-repository compatibility.

uncertainty, sample comparability, causal validity, mechanism validity,
predictive generalization, optimization readiness, or production engineering
readiness.
