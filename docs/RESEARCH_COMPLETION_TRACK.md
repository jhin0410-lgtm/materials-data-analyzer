# Research completion track

This document tracks the repository's transition from a provenance-aware research platform into a bounded autonomous research scientist that can complete real research episodes without weakening evidence standards.

Priority order:

1. Evidence federation: authoritative raw/processed datasets, supplementary data, publication tables/figures, literature claims, computational and reference evidence remain separate evidence classes.
2. Persistent research episodes: deterministic checkpoint/resume over existing authenticated ledgers and transitions.
3. Human scientific review unlock: SHA-pinned review artifacts may release only explicitly reviewed downstream uses.
4. Sample/experiment/acquisition/measurement lineage: prevent pseudoreplication and false independence.
5. Research-agent benchmark: measure false evidence promotion, abstention, provenance completeness, independence handling, action quality and cost efficiency.
6. Advanced statistics: DOE readiness, variance components, mixed-effects/hierarchical eligibility and measurement-uncertainty propagation.
7. Literature evidence: Crossref/DataCite/PMC and repository adapters, supplementary/table/figure/narrative provenance.
8. EIG/simulation: probabilistic information gain only when a validated probabilistic model supports it; otherwise preserve structural proxies.
9. Minimal research operations UI: inspect episode state, evidence gaps, review queue, provenance and next actions without becoming a second execution authority.

Completion is not tied to exact NIST issue #76. Exact empirical blockers remain unresolved where evidence does not exist, while independent adjacent research episodes should continue using appropriately graded evidence.

## Completion criterion

An Autonomous Research Scientist MVP requires at least three materially different real-data episodes to execute question -> evidence acquisition -> scientific intake -> analysis -> weakness/contradiction detection -> next-action selection -> reanalysis -> bounded stop without code modification, while preserving provenance and refusing unsupported scientific promotion.
