# Battery v2.3.5 Scientific Boundary

Status: `bounded_descriptive_stability_evidence_only`

v2.3.5 supports these claims:

- exact lineage from 34 trajectories to the verified local Kaggle package;
- exact source-key recovery of supported metadata for 2,495 analysis-ready
  discharge rows;
- deterministic sensitivity under nine predeclared evaluator policies;
- consolidation of overlapping findings into bounded descriptive events;
- explicit classification of event stability and policy dependence.

It does not support degradation-mechanism identification, physical transition
confirmation, mechanism parameter fitting, SOH/RUL/lifetime prediction,
causal temperature or protocol effects, calibrated uncertainty, or production
decisions. Stable findings remain descriptive patterns. Policy-sensitive
findings must not be interpreted as physical changes.

The immediate Kaggle package is verified; the authoritative original NASA
snapshot/version is not. Timestamps and duration evidence do not convert the
cycle-index evaluator into a physical-time model. Group-level protocol notes
do not supply cycle-specific commanded conditions. Missing uncertainty is
`unavailable`, not zero.

The v2.2 decisions and the v2.3.1 through v2.3.4 decisions are checksum-
preserved. In particular, the v2.3.4 decision remains
`descriptive_evaluator_executed_with_restrictions`, and no representative
mechanism is selected.
